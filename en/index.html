Bridge Cantata

9 May 2026 - The Bridge Cantata

The project took place at the Eđšeg Cultural Station in Novi Sad, bringing together the early music ensemble Musica Antiqua Neoplantensis, dancer Tatjana Stricki, composer David Mastikosa, and dramaturg Mina Petric.

Following the workshop held on 18 April at American Corner, the project page now works as a record of the process, the performance, and the materials available so far.

Since 2012

Baroque Music Days

Baroque Music Days is a festival programme that VEMA has been developing since 2012, bringing together performers, ensembles, and audiences around early and baroque music in Novi Sad. Across different editions, the festival builds continuity, collaboration, and a recognizable identity.

ABOUT US

Vojvodinian Early Music Association (VEMA) is a platform for historically informed performance and education, rooted in local heritage and connected to European networks.

Mission

Reviving and presenting early music through high-quality concerts, masterclasses, and research, while strengthening cultural life in Novi Sad and Vojvodina.

What We Do

  • Produce concerts and festival programmes in historical spaces
  • Provide education, workshops, and support for young musicians
  • Develop international collaborations and cultural heritage projects
